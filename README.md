# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, drawn
entirely in code (`draw.py`, PIL) and copied to the output by `main.py`.

## The day

- **AI rout hits Asia** — Kospi −3.3% (closes 4,708.54, sheds 161 pts,
  intraday −5.8%); Samsung −3.1%, SK Hynix −2.5%.
- **Guterres: regulate AI before it's too late** — UN chief urges global AI
  safety rules at the Bridgetown summit; 150 delegates back a risk framework.
- **US lead over China at record low** — Asia Power Index 2026: US 78.9,
  China 73.6, gap 5.3, narrowest since 2018; India third at 40.5.

31 stories across 6 regions, with a number strip, a "THE DAY IN UTC" strip,
a world map with 31 numbered pins, an "ALSO ON MONDAY" list, and a DATE CHECK
box naming what was left out (and why) and what is not yet known.

## Files

- `draw.py` — draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) with PIL only.
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
  (stdlib only; never writes next to itself).
- `entry.png` — the image.

Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs. Times marked `~` are
approximate; one-sided claims are labelled CLAIM.
